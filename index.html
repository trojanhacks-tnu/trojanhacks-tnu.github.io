The Call

Most class projects end when the semester does. Trojan Hacks is a chance to build something real — start to finish, in one sitting, with people outside your own major.

It's a space to learn by doing instead of by studying, to try tools and ideas that don't fit into a syllabus, and to walk away with something you actually built — something worth putting in a portfolio.
