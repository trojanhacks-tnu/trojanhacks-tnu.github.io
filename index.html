08 / Know Before You Enter

Know Before You Enter.

Everything you need before the gates open.

What is Trojan Hacks?

Trojan Hacks is a six-hour, in-person hackathon at Trevecca Nazarene University where students work in teams to build software, hardware, tools, experiments, and creative projects from scratch.

Who can participate?

Trojan Hacks 2026 is open to undergraduate and graduate students. Students from different schools, majors, and experience levels can participate. Additional eligibility details will be shared before registration opens.

Do I need hackathon experience?

No. Previous hackathon experience is not required. First-time hackers are welcome.

What if I do not have a team?

Team formation details are still being finalized. We plan to make it possible for students to meet other participants and form teams around the event.

What does it cost?

Cost and registration details are still being finalized and will be announced soon.

What should I bring?

A complete packing list and technical preparation guide will be shared closer to the event.

What can I build?

Teams will build a project during the hackathon. Final tracks and challenge details will be announced as sponsors and event programming are confirmed.
