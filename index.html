Build Beyond The Classroom.

Most class projects end when the semester does. Trojan Hacks is six hours to build something real — start to finish, with people outside your own major.

You learn by doing: trying things, getting them wrong, and figuring it out as you go. Whatever you build is yours to keep — proof of work for a portfolio, not just a grade.
